Job eligibility mirrors Vanai::Data.eligible_jobs, the one definition the equip endpoint also reads: weapons match a job's weapon_type against the item's wtype; head/body/legs/feet match the job's armor list against atype, with atype: "any" meaning every job may wear it. Rings, consumables and materials carry no restriction. Every armour piece states its class explicitly — the build fails if one does not. DPS is dmg / delay, shown for reference only. A dash under Gil means the item has no shop price (the server refuses to sell anything at 0); sell marks a crafting material priced only for what a vendor pays you.
