# Moonmere furniture and door modules

Every GLB in this directory is original project geometry created with
`tools/assets/build_moonmere_modules.py` (the editable Blender library it saves
is `design/moonmere-city/modules.blend`). No third-party mesh, texture, or
other content was used to make these modules.

These 16 are what is left of the old Moonmere's module set: the furniture and
the arched door leaf the game still places (the annex door's leaf, the
shelves, stamp desks and observatory instruments, Heartwood's homes and stalls,
the Sunken Ballroom's music box). The old city's architecture -- its walls,
windows, roofs, stairs, floors, quay and lamps -- was retired with it on
2026-10-09 (MOONMERE.md "The old city, retired"), and the door's frame
(`wall_door`) on 2026-10-10, once the Long Tower framed that door itself. The
new city's modules are
in `../moonmere-cloister/`.

The modules fit Vanai's 2 m construction grid and 3.125 m storey. Their
materials are flat colours on the GLB; no runtime plugin restyles them.
