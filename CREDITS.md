# Credits

Everything original to Vanai is MIT ([LICENSE](LICENSE)): the code, and
everything made in this repository -- the generated creatures, the cities' and
dungeons' modules and props, the dungeon textures, the baked backdrops, the
synthesized score and sound effects, the procedural icons, and the world, story
and game data in `shared/data/`. The third-party work below is the only
exception, and keeps its own licence.

The character runtime (bodies, hair, gear, weapons and animations; see
[ASSETS.md](docs/ASSETS.md)) is a separate folder that ships with its own README,
CREDITS.md and LICENSE. It is MIT as well, except its Quaternius Universal
Animation Library clips, which are CC0.

## Textures and lighting

All from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), CC0 ([their licence](https://polyhaven.com/license)).

- `client/public/tex/`: aerial_beach_01 (Rob Tuytel), aerial_rocks_02,
  brown_mud_leaves_01, cobblestone_floor_08, dry_ground_01, dry_riverbed_rock,
  forest_leaves_03, forrest_ground_03, leafy_grass, mossy_cobblestone, snow_02,
  wood_floor_worn. Each name is its Poly Haven slug,
  `https://polyhaven.com/a/<name>`.
- `client/public/tex/build/`: dark_wooden_planks, grey_plaster_02,
  medieval_blocks_02, roof_slates_02, wood_floor_worn.
- `client/public/env/sky_1k.hdr`: [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky)
  by Greg Zaal, sky edits by Jarod Guest, at 1k; it lights the scene and is not
  the sky you see. Identified on 2026-10-18 by matching it against every Poly
  Haven HDRI preview (0.99 correlation; the next best was 0.80).

`client/public/tex/dungeon/` is original, generated by
`tools/assets/gen_dungeon_textures.py`.

## Vendored code

`client/public/ktx2/` holds the KTX2 texture decoder, fetched from Babylon's CDN
(see its [README](client/public/ktx2/README.md)):

- `babylon.ktx2Decoder.js`, `msc_basis_transcoder.*` and the `uastc_*.wasm`
  transcoders: [Babylon.js](https://github.com/BabylonJS/Babylon.js) and
  [Basis Universal](https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal), Apache-2.0
  ([licence text](client/public/ktx2/LICENSE-Apache-2.0.md)).
- `zstddec.wasm`: the Zstandard decompressor, from
  `https://cdn.babylonjs.com/zstddec.wasm`, built by
  [zstddec](https://github.com/donmccurdy/zstddec) (Don McCurdy, MIT) from
  [Zstandard](https://github.com/facebook/zstd) (Meta Platforms, BSD-3-Clause)
  ([both notices](client/public/ktx2/LICENSE-zstddec.txt)). It unpacks the
  zstd-compressed character and gear textures.

npm and Ruby dependencies are listed in `client/package-lock.json` and
`server/Gemfile.lock`, each under its own licence.
